### **Disclaimer of Opinion**

We were engaged to audit the standalone financial statements of Cameo Global Communications Private Limited, which comprise the Balance Sheet as at March 31, 2023, the statement of Profit and Loss, and the Cash Flow Statement for the year then ended, and notes to the financial statements, including a summary of significant accounting policies information (hereinafter referred to as "the standalone financial statements")

# **Basis for Disclaimer of Opinion**

During the FY 22-23, **Cameo Global Communications Private Limited** (*Purchaser*) entered into a Business Transfer Agreement (BTA) dated 7<sup>th</sup> April 2023, with **Aptude Software Solutions India Private Limited**, (*seller*) where the purchaser has taken over the business undertaking, as a going concern on a slump sale basis effective date of the transaction being 1<sup>st</sup> April 2023, for a sale consideration of **INR 300 lakhs**, in accordance with terms and conditions outlined in BTA.

Further, we were not made available the Purchase Price Allocation report for the said transaction to review the assets and liabilities forming part of the deal and its impact on the books of accounts. Accordingly, we are unable to comment on the completeness of the transaction and its related impact on the financial statements including recognition of goodwill in the Company's books.
