# ZENOX GAS SYSTEMS PRIVATE LIMITED SIGNIFICANT ACCOUNTING POLICIES As at 31st March 2026 STATEMENT OF SIGNIFICANT ACCOUNTING POLICIES

#### (1) Background of the company.

Zenox Gas Systems Private Limited was incorporated on 06<sup>th</sup> August, 2025 having the business object of "To design, manufacture, assemble, install, commission, and maintain gas systems, including but not limited to industrial gas generation systems, on-site gas plants, and packaged gas supply solutions for industrial and medical use. To fabricate, and deal in filtration systems, air purification units, gas drying systems, and related components for various industrial, pharmaceutical, medical, and environmental applications." Its registered office at Kosmo One, Tower B, First Floor, 3<sup>rd</sup> Main Road, Ambattur Industrial Estate, Ambattur, Chennai - 600058.

The company is a Small and Medium Size Company (SME) as defined in the general instructions in respect of accounting standard notified under the Companies Act, 2013. Accordingly, the company has complied with accounting standards as applicable to Small and Medium Sized Company (SME) (URN – UDYAM-TN-02-0423275).

#### A1. Accounting Convention

These accounts have been prepared under the historic cost convention on the basis of Going Concern and comply in all material aspects with applicable accounting principles in India, the Accounting Standards issued by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India and the relevant provisions of the Companies Act, 2013.

The preparation of financial statements in conformity with Generally Accepted Accounting Principles requires making of estimates and assumptions that effect the reported amounts of assets and liabilities and disclosure of contingent assets and liabilities at the date of financial statements and the reported amounts of revenues and expenses during the reporting year. Any revision to accounting estimates is recognized prospectively in current and future periods. Difference between the actual results and estimates are known/materialized.

#### A2. Fixed Assets

Fixed Assets are stated at historical cost including any directly attributable expenses incurred (less taxes recoverable) to bring the assets to working condition for their intended use less accumulated depreciation.

#### A3. Depreciation

Depreciation has been provided on "Written down Value" at the rates specified in Schedule II of the Companies Act, 2013. Assets purchased/sold during the year are depreciated on a pro rata basis for the actual number of Months the asset has been put to use.

Amortization will be charged for purchase of technology over the period of 5 years of useful life. Lease hold improvements shall be charged over the life term of the lease

#### A4. Revenue Recognition

Revenue is recognized when no significant uncertainty as to the determination or realization exists.

Revenue from the sale of goods is recognized upon the delivery of goods to customers.

Revenue from services is recognized as and when services are rendered and related cost are incurred, in accordance with the terms of the specific contracts.

#### A5. Impairment of Assets:

At each balance sheet date, the Company reviews the carrying amounts of its fixed assets to determine whether there is any indication that those assets suffered an impairment loss based on internal/external factors. If any such indication exists, the recoverable amount of the asset is estimated in order to determine the extent of impairment lose.

Recoverable amount is the higher of an asset's net selling price and value in use. In assessing value in use, the estimated future cash flows expected from the continuing use of the asset and from its disposal are discounted to their present value using a pre-tax discount rate that reflects the current market assessments of time value of money and the risks specific to the asset.

Reversal of impairment loss is recognized immediately as income in the profit and loss account.

#### A6. Provisions, Contingent Liability and contingent Assets:

In accordance with AS-29 issued by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India, Company recognizes Provisions when it has a present obligation as a result of past events and it is probable that outflow of resources embodying economic benefits will be required to settle the obligation and reliable estimate can be made.

Provisions are determined based on technical evaluation and management estimates required to settle the obligation at the balance sheet date, supplemented by past experience of similar transactions. These are reviewed at each balance sheet date and adjusted to reflect the current management estimates.

A disclosure for contingent liability is made when there is possible obligation or a present obligation that may but probable will not require an out flow of resources. When there is a possible obligation or a present obligation in respect of which the likelihood off outflow of resources is remote. No provision or disclosure is made. Contingent assets are not recognized in the financial statements.

Provisions and contingencies are reviewed at each balance sheet dates and adjusted to reflect the correct management estimates.

#### A7. Investments

Investments are stated at cost and income thereon credited to revenue on accrual basis.

#### A11. Taxation

Provision for current tax is made on the basis of the assessable income at the rates applicable to the relevant assessment year in accordance with the Income Tax Act, 1961.
